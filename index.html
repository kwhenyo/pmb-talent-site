About

A staffing partner built on relationships, not requisitions.

PMB Talent Partners is a minority-owned staffing agency dedicated to promoting diversity and inclusion in every placement it makes. The goal is straightforward: become the go-to staffing partner in every market served, earned through client excellence and relationships built to last — not one-off fills.

Services

Four ways to bring on talent.

Every engagement is customized — whether the need is a single permanent hire or a project-based team.

01 / DIRECT HIRE

Direct Hire Placements

Permanent, full-time placements sourced and vetted against your team's actual working needs — not just a job description.

02 / CONTRACT-TO-HIRE

Contract-to-Hire

A working trial period for both sides. Bring someone on under contract, convert to permanent once the fit is proven.

03 / CONTRACT STAFFING

Contract Staffing

Skilled talent for defined projects, seasonal surges, or coverage gaps — scaled up or down as the work requires.

04 / CUSTOM SOLUTIONS

Customized Solutions

Permanent and project-based staffing plans built around a specific operation, shift pattern, or hiring timeline.
